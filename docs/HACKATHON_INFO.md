## 4. Eligibility & Team Rules

- Open to **anyone who can build products** — beginners to seasoned builders
- **Apply individually.** There are no team applications; **each person is evaluated individually**
- **Collaboration is encouraged** — multiple people can work on the same project, but **each person submits their own work**
- You must actually **build and deploy** something (not a deck, not a prototype mockup)

> ⚠️ Implication for us: if we work as a group, every member applies separately and submits separately. Plan for individually-attributable contributions and commit history.

## 6. Submission Requirements (Due 10 Aug 2026)

### Deliverables
1. **Deployed app** — web or mobile
2. **Screencast** — demonstrating the build process

### Technical checklist (from the site, verbatim)

- [ ] **A clear purpose** — "solves a real problem; obvious what it does and who it's for"
- [ ] **Built with AI** — "AI apps, AI agents, or AI woven into the product"
- [ ] **A usable UI** — "looks good and is easy to use"
- [ ] **Responsive** — "works well on laptop and phone"
- [ ] **User login** — "real authentication, not a mock-up"
- [ ] **A real backend** — "saves data, so it behaves like a real product"
- [ ] **Deployed & live** — "a live URL for web (e.g. Vercel), or an installable build"
- [ ] **Open source & version control** — "a public repo with real commit history"
- [ ] **Nice extras** *(optional)* — "email, chat, or social integrations"

> 🔴 **Hard requirements that kill submissions:** real auth (not fake), a real persistent backend, a live public URL, and a **public repo with genuine commit history**. Start committing early and often — history is explicitly judged.
>
> 📢 **All submissions must be open source.**

## 7. Judging Criteria

Evaluation emphasizes:

- Genuine **positive impact**
- Solving a **real problem** (explicitly *not* purely commercial)
- **AI integration quality**
- **UI/UX polish**
- **Technical completeness**
- **Responsiveness** across devices
- **Authentic user authentication**
- **Data persistence**
- **Live deployment**
- **Version control visibility**

Additionally: a **workflow demo and follow-up interview** are part of prize consideration.

### "What earns a seat in Dharamshala" — the *human* criteria

From the Build Season page. These sit alongside the 9 technical items and are **not** about code:

> *"It's not about pedigree or a perfect résumé — it's about how you build, and how you show up for the people building alongside you."*

| Criterion | Site wording |
|---|---|
| **Consistency** | "You showed up and shipped — daily updates, steady progress, **not a last-night sprint**." |
| **A working app** | "Something real and deployed — a good UI, works on phone and laptop, ideally with login and a real backend." |
| **Originality** | "A real idea, approached in a way that **isn't the obvious one**." |
| **Helpfulness** | "You lifted the people around you — unblocked others, shared what you learned." |
| **Your workflow** | "How you actually build with AI — prompting, debugging, thinking. Share it as a short demo; it's the clearest window into your craft." |
| **Seriousness** | "You're here to build something real, not to collect a line on a résumé." |

> ⚠️ **Three of these six are earned in Discord, not in the editor** — daily updates (Consistency), helping others (Helpfulness), and showing your process (Workflow). A perfect app submitted silently on 10 Aug scores worse than a good app built in public. Start posting daily updates immediately.
>
> ⚠️ **Originality is explicitly judged** — "isn't the obvious one". This argues against the most predictable Idea Wall picks.

## 12. The Fellowship (Post-Hackathon)

- **What:** a **3-month** program that turns a hackathon demo into a functional product with real users
- **Eligibility:** must have participated in Build Season **with a working demo**; selective, based on demonstrated progress
- **Application:** *"There's no cold application."* Applications open right after the season's **Demo Day** and require: progress, early traction, learnings, roadmap, and an updated demo
- **Benefits:**
  - Funding & time for focused development (amount not published)
  - Mentorship from experienced builders — technical and business
  - User access via NGO and impact partners
  - A small cohort of builders working on meaningful problems
- **"Done" looks like:** committed collaborators, a working product beyond prototype, real users or a credible path to them, and a defined go-to-market strategy
